What a probiotic label can tell you

Look for the genus, species and strain on the label, along with the use-by date and storage instructions. These details help you understand what you’re buying and how to keep it.

Source: NIH Office of Dietary Supplements

Hand care starts after washing

Pat your hands dry, then apply a cream while the skin is still slightly damp. Moisturizer helps hold on to that water. Keeping it beside the sink makes the step easier to remember.

Source: American Academy of Dermatology
